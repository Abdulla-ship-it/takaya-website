Our approach

Small cohorts. Real practice. Measured progress.

We built Takaya because most training in the region ends when the certificate is printed. Our programmes are designed backwards from the change you want to see — then supported until it holds.

01

Taught in small groups

Cohorts are capped so every participant is known by name, challenged directly, and given room to contribute.

02

Built on live work

You apply each module to a real project from your own role. You leave with work completed, not notes taken.

03

Supported after the room

Every programme includes follow-up coaching sessions, so new habits survive contact with a normal working week.
