One-to-one coaching

A dedicated coach, and a plan built only for you.

Coaching at Takaya is confidential, structured and outcome-led. You are matched with a coach, you agree what success looks like in session one, and every session after that moves you towards it.

How it works

Four steps, no mystery

01

Intro call

A free 20-minute conversation to understand what you're facing and whether coaching is the right tool.

02

Coach matching

We match you to the coach whose background fits your situation — not simply whoever is free.

03

Session one defines the outcome, the measures, and the boundaries of what's confidential.

04

The work

Fortnightly sessions with agreed actions between each, plus messaging access to your coach.

Who this is for

Coaching works best when something specific is at stake.

If you're generally content and simply curious, a course will serve you better and cost less. Coaching earns its place when there's a real decision, transition or pattern you need to work through — and you want someone in your corner who has no agenda but yours.

You've been promoted and it's harder than expected

The skills that got you here are not the ones the new role needs.

You're circling a decision you can't settle

Leave or stay, start something or don't — and the loop has become the problem.

You're producing at a cost you can't sustain

The output still looks fine from outside. You know what it's taking.

You want to grow but have no honest mirror

Nobody around you is positioned to tell you the truth about your work.
