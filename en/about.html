About the institute

A space where people discover they were always bigger than they thought.

Takaya is an Omani institute specialised in human and professional development, founded on the conviction that real training is never added to a person from the outside — it awakens what was always there.

Who we are

An Omani institute for human and professional development

The institute serves three audiences: individuals seeking personal growth, employees looking to develop their career paths, and organisations seeking to build more capable, more effective teams.

We deliver our programmes through a methodology that combines theoretical depth with practical application, and we carry a different voice in the Omani market — one that speaks to the person before it speaks to the employee, believing that skill alone isn't enough unless it grows from a person who knows themselves and trusts what they carry.

Takaya isn't just a training institute. It's a space where people come to discover they were always bigger than they thought.

Our mission

We seek to empower people with knowledge, skill and values, through an inspiring training environment that brings together professional development and human growth.

Who founded Takaya

Founding Partners

A partnership of human vision and field experience.

Her Highness Sayyida Zayana Al Said

Founding Partner, Takaya Institute

Since graduating from Sultan Qaboos University in 2006, she has carried a passion for building an educational project that creates positive impact in society, continuing her own development through numerous courses and programmes that built her intellectual, administrative and personal skills. Her experience with a specialised coach and consultant marked a major turning point, helping her discover her true passion and latent abilities, and she came to understand the importance of a person grasping their essence and applying their potential to build a more meaningful, more impactful life. From this vision, her founding partnership in the "Takaya" project for training and empowerment was born — aiming to help individuals discover their abilities, refine their skills and achieve positive impact in their lives and communities.

Abeer Al-Amoureyah

Founding Partner, Takaya Institute

She holds more than twenty years of experience in training and human development, combining field experience with specialised knowledge in NLP, personality-type sciences and multiple intelligences, and train-the-trainer work, brought together in a distinctive methodology she delivers through Takaya Club. She studied the human sciences in historic Alexandria on the Mediterranean coast, and graduated to spread confidence in people's capacity to grow and change through precise, scientific methods. Her training style combines depth with simplicity, focused on helping individuals discover their potential and turn it into tangible achievements — leaving an influential mark on programmes and initiatives across her homeland and beyond.
