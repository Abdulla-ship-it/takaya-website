About the institute

We teach the way we wish we had been taught.

Takaya was founded in Muscat on a simple frustration: professional training in the region too often ends the moment the certificate is printed. We set out to build the opposite.

Our story

Built for how people actually learn.

Most of us have sat through a three-day workshop, filled a notebook, felt genuinely inspired — and then changed nothing at all. Not because the content was wrong, but because nothing in the design of the training expected us to use it.

Takaya is structured around the opposite assumption. Cohorts are small enough that no one hides. Every module is applied to real work from your own role. And every programme includes coaching afterwards, because the gap between knowing and doing is where almost all learning is lost.

We work with individuals building a career, and with organisations across Oman developing their people. The format changes. The standard does not.

What we hold to

Four commitments

01

Honesty over sales

If a programme isn't right for you, we will say so — including when the answer is that you don't need us.

02

Practice over theory

Frameworks earn their place only when they change what you do on a Sunday morning.

03

Small over scale

We cap cohorts deliberately. Growth that dilutes the room is growth we decline.

04

Rooted in Oman

Built here, taught here, in the languages and contexts our participants actually work in.
